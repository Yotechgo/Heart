Parameter Risk Guidelines & Interpretation

Low Risk Parameters

These parameters represent standard healthy ranges or trigger the automatic low-risk system override.

Parameter Value / Range Clinical Description
Age < 100 years Baseline evaluation age under 100 years.
Resting BP (trestbps) ≤ 120 mmHg Normal resting systolic blood pressure. Values within this range indicate healthy blood vessels.
Serum Cholesterol (chol) ≤ 200 mg/dL Desirable total cholesterol level. Minimizes risk of arterial plaque buildup.
Fasting Blood Sugar (fbs) No (≤ 120 mg/dL) Normal fasting glucose range. Indicates no active hyperglycemic stress.
Exercise Angina (exang) No (0) No chest pain or constriction brought on by physical exercise.
ST Depression (oldpeak) 0.0 - 3.0 Minimal ST segment depression. Indicates normal cardiac electrical activity under stress.
Major Vessels (ca) 0 No coronary vessels showing stenosis/blockage under fluoroscopy.
Chest Pain (cp) 0 Asymptomatic chest pain (no subjective anginal symptoms).
Rest ECG (restecg) 0 Normal electrocardiographic reading at rest.
Max Heart Rate (thalach) > 150 bpm Excellent cardiac performance and cardiovascular fitness.
ST Slope 2 Upsloping ST segment (typical healthy response to exercise exertion).
Thalassemia (thal) 1 Normal blood flow perfusion scan without defects.

High Risk Parameters

Parameters representing critical threat levels or triggering the automatic high-risk system override.

Parameter Value / Range Clinical Description
Resting BP (trestbps) ≥ 180 mmHg Hypertensive crisis level. Triggers automatic high-risk classification.
Serum Cholesterol (chol) ≥ 260 mg/dL Severely high cholesterol. Associated with extensive atherosclerosis. Triggers high-risk override.
Exercise Angina (exang) Yes (1) Chest pain/angina induced directly by exercise. Highly diagnostic of heart disease. Triggers high-risk override.
ST Depression (oldpeak) 6.0 - 10.0 Severe ST depression indicating significant myocardial ischemia. Triggers high-risk override.
Major Vessels (ca) ≥ 2 Multiple major blood vessels blocked. High urgency condition. Triggers high-risk override.
Chest Pain (cp) 2 - 3 Typical severe angina chest pain or non-anginal pain (higher disease correlation).
Rest ECG (restecg) 2 Left ventricular hypertrophy (severe wave abnormality or strain).
Max Heart Rate (thalach) < 120 bpm Poor cardiovascular exertion capacity under stress.
ST Slope 0 Downsloping ST segment slope (strong marker of active ischemia).
Thalassemia (thal) 3 (Reversible defect) Indicates temporary loss of blood flow to cardiac muscle during exertion.
